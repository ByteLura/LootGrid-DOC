Before you write

Run Verify Install first (workshop welcome block, or Tools > ByteLura > LootGrid Pro > Verify Install) — its lines check the assemblies, exercise the model in memory, and confirm the sample and documentation are present. The Troubleshooting page covers the common questions it answers.

What to include

Include your Unity version and the Verify Install lines; they answer most questions before the second email.
